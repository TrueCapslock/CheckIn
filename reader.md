# CheckIn - User Guide

Welcome to **CheckIn**, a location-based check-in app that lets you discover, visit, and share your favorite places with friends!

## 🎯 What is CheckIn?

CheckIn is an interactive app that encourages you to explore locations, check in to places you visit, earn rewards, and compete with friends. Whether you're into bars, restaurants, cafes, clubs, lounges, parks, or things to do, CheckIn helps you track your adventures and build a social community around shared experiences.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Create Your Profile

1. **Sign In**: Visit [checkin.hartvig.info](https://checkin.hartvig.info) and log in with your email
2. **Set Your Name**: Choose a username to display to other users
3. **Add an Avatar**: Upload a profile picture to personalize your account (stored as a data URL)

Your profile tracks:
- **Coins**: Currency earned from check-ins
- **Points**: Your cumulative score
- **Stickers**: Badges for visiting different place types
- **Achievements**: Special milestones you've unlocked
- **Streaks**: Your check-in consistency records

## 🗺️ Discovering Places

### Browse Categories

Places are organized into 7 categories:

- 🍸 **Bars** - Craft breweries, beer bars, and taverns
- 🍽️ **Restaurants** - Dining establishments with full menus
- ☕ **Cafes** - Coffee shops and casual eateries
- 🎵 **Clubs** - Music venues and nightlife
- 🥂 **Lounges** - Upscale cocktail lounges
- 🌳 **Parks** - Outdoor recreation and green spaces
- 🎪 **Things to Do** - Activities, events, and attractions

### Search for Places

1. Use the **map view** to visually browse nearby locations
2. Use the **search bar** to find specific places by name
3. Filter by **category** to narrow your options
4. Check the **distance** (within 100 meters for valid check-ins)

## ✅ Check-Ing In

### How to Check In

1. **Find a Place**: Locate a place on the map or in search results
2. **View Details**: Tap to see the place's info (address, phone, hours, website)
3. **Check In**: Press the check-in button when you're at the location
4. **Earn Rewards**: You'll receive **10 points** per check-in

### Location Requirements

- You must be **within 100 meters** of the place to check in
- The app uses your device's GPS to verify your location
- This prevents fake check-ins from the other side of town!

### Upload Photos

After checking in, you can:
- Upload **photos of the place** from your device
- Help other users see what the location looks like
- Build a community gallery for each place

## 👥 Social Features

### Follow Friends

1. **Search for Friends**: Find users by their username
2. **Follow**: Click follow to add them to your network
3. **View Their Activity**: See their recent check-ins and stats

### View Leaderboards

- **Personal Stats**: Track your own coins, points, and streaks
- **Friend Comparisons**: See how you stack up against friends
- **Global Rankings**: Compete on app-wide leaderboards (coming soon)

## 🎉 Parties (Group Check-In Events)

### Create a Party

1. **Start a New Party**: Give it a name and set a time window
2. **Invite Friends**: Add friends by their usernames
3. **Check In Together**: All members check in to the same place during the party
4. **Earn Group Rewards**: Bonus points for group participation

### Join a Party

1. **Browse Active Parties**: See parties near you or created by friends
2. **Request to Join**: Ask for an invite
3. **Check In with the Group**: Visit the location during the party window
4. **See Live Activity**: Watch real-time updates as members join

## 📊 Your Profile & Stats

### Profile Dashboard

Your profile displays:
- **Username** and avatar
- **Coins** earned
- **Points** total
- **Sticker Collections** - Visual badges for each category visited
- **Achievement Badges** - Special milestones unlocked
- **Check-In Streaks** - Consecutive days of check-ins per category

### Streaks

- Track your **consecutive check-ins** in each category
- Maintain your streak by checking in regularly
- Higher streaks unlock special badges and bonus points

## 🎁 Rewards System

### How You Earn Rewards

- **Base Check-In**: +10 points per visit
- **Group Check-Ins**: Bonus points for party participation
- **Streaks**: Extra rewards for consistency
- **Achievements**: Unlock special badges with milestones

### Sticker Collection

Collect stickers by visiting places in each category. Your collection is displayed on your profile!

## 🗺️ Using the Map

### Map Controls

- **Zoom In/Out**: Explore specific neighborhoods or see the full city
- **Pan**: Drag to move around
- **Markers**: Each pin represents a place
- **Click Markers**: View place details and check-in option

### Location Services

- Grant **location permission** when prompted
- The app uses GPS to show your current position
- Accuracy is needed for check-in distance verification

## ⚙️ Settings & Preferences

- **Privacy**: Control who can see your profile and check-ins

- **Max Check-In Distance**: 100 meters (app-wide setting)

- **Responsive Design**: Works on phones, tablets, and desktop

- **Fast Loading**: Maps and search optimized for 4G/5G

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### "Cannot Check In" Message

- ✅ Verify you're within **100 meters** of the location
- ✅ Enable **location services** on your device
- ✅ Check your phone's GPS accuracy

### Photos Not Uploading

- ✅ Check your **internet connection**
- ✅ Verify the **image size** isn't too large
- ✅ Try a different image format

### Can't Find a Place

- ✅ Try **searching by exact name**
- ✅ Check **category filters**
- ✅ Ensure **location permission** is enabled
- ✅ The place might not be in the database yet—add it!

### Lost Connection to Friends

- ✅ Refresh the **leaderboard** page
- ✅ Check your **friend list** for current follows
- ✅ Verify friend's **username** is spelled correctly

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

1. **Complete Categories**: Visit all places in one category to earn special category stickers
2. **Time Your Streaks**: Check in once daily to build streaks in your favorite categories
3. **Create Themed Parties**: Organize friend groups for specific venue types
4. **Upload Quality Photos**: Help your community by sharing clear, well-lit photos of places
5. **Follow Local Explorers**: Follow other users to discover places you haven't visited yet
6. **Check Party Notifications**: Get alerts when friends create parties near you
